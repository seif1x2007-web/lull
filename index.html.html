Before you drift off

Questions, answered.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes. Pay at your door when your Lull arrives — no card needed. We currently deliver across Cairo, Giza, and Alexandria, with more cities coming.

How fast is delivery?

Usually 1–3 days in Greater Cairo and Alexandria. We confirm your order over WhatsApp before it ships, so nothing arrives as a surprise.

Is the mouth tape safe?

Our tape is a soft, vented strip designed to gently encourage nasal breathing — not seal your mouth shut. It isn't suitable for anyone with significant nasal congestion, breathing conditions, or sleep apnea. If that's you, use the mask and strips and skip the tape, or check with your doctor first.

What if I don't like it?

Free returns within Cairo. If the Kit isn't for you, message us on WhatsApp and we'll sort it.

How do refills work?

Strips and tape are nightly, so they run out. Subscribe once and a fresh pack arrives monthly, paid on delivery. Skip or cancel any month with a single WhatsApp message.
